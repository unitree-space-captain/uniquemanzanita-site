Manzanita grows crooked. No two branches take the same shape, and the length printed on a label tells U almost nothing about whether a piece will sit in ur tank. So we measure the box each piece occupies, publish it, and let U buy against the space U actually have.

What an envelope is

The maximum external box a piece may occupy, measured at the widest point on each axis rather than along its longest branch. It is the number that decides whether a piece clears a bracing bar, and it is the number that sets the shipping charge, because size rather than weight prices most parcels.

Why the figures are provisional

Each envelope is confirmed after the packing trial for that grade, and the confirmed figure becomes the one U are quoted against. The indicative prices are test values used to size the offer. They are not quotations, and no grade has been released for sale.

For the layout U have already drawn

The one U saw is the one U get.

Some pieces are too particular to describe as a size class. Those are photographed from the front, the side and above, measured in three dimensions, given a serial number, and sold once.

One physical item, one inventory record, quantity one. When a serial sells it is marked sold rather than quietly replaced with something similar. Every measurement is taken at the widest point on that axis, not the longest reach of a branch.

Where it came from

One stand, in the Yuba foothills.

This page is a design preview. It is not a store. No product has been released, no piece has been offered for sale, and no order can be placed.

Every figure is a working assumption. Grade envelopes, case packs, the specimen record and all prices are planning values held until preparation costs are measured and packing trials are run. They are not quotations.

No botanical name appears here on purpose. The species has not been determined on the ground by a qualified botanist. Nothing on this page states or implies one.

No suitability or safety claim is made for any animal, species or enclosure. Care and preparation instructions will be published with the released product, and they will describe what was actually done to the wood rather than what it is guaranteed to do.
