Example assortment: 15 pieces total. This is a planning example, not a confirmed case pack or an offer for sale. The number of shipping cartons is still to be determined.

The wood, the package and the case.

These are three different measurements. A piece's overall length, width and height describe the room the wood occupies. Retail package dimensions describe its display space. Packed case dimensions describe the shipment. We plan to list each separately.

Pricing for U and ur customers.

The planned trade sheet will show wholesale prices per piece and assortment, suggested retail prices and freight terms. Every price and dimension remains provisional until preparation costs are measured and packing trials are complete. No figures are quotations.

Special orders through ur store

When a customer needs a larger piece.

Keep the standard sizes on ur shelves and offer a separate route for larger requests. Our planned service would let U share a customer's available space, preferred shape and quantity, then review individual pieces subject to availability.

Before an order is agreed, U would receive photographs of the proposed piece, its overall dimensions, a wholesale quote, packing and freight details, and an estimated lead time. Natural shapes vary; a request does not guarantee a matching piece. Special orders are not open yet.

Our California source

From the Yuba foothills.

UNIque Manzanita Company is based in Brownsville, Yuba County, California. Our proposed wholesale range starts with manzanita from a single local source, with batch records planned to connect each piece to its preparation history.

This page is a design preview. It is not a store. No product has been released, no piece has been offered for sale, and no order can be placed.

Every figure is a working assumption. Size limits, case assortments, package dimensions and all prices are planning values held until preparation costs are measured and packing trials are run. They are not quotations.

No botanical name appears here on purpose. The species has not been determined on the ground by a qualified botanist. Nothing on this page states or implies one.

No suitability or safety claim is made for any animal, species or enclosure. Care and preparation instructions will be published with the released product, and they will describe what was actually done to the wood rather than what it is guaranteed to do.
